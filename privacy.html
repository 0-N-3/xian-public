Privacy — Xian Personal Archive

Xian Personal Archive is operated for its owner's personal use. This notice describes the file-copy utility, not every other Xian tool or connected service.

Data accessed. The utility processes saved files in configured local archive folders and the Drive metadata needed to identify and verify recovery copies, including names, paths, sizes, identifiers and checksums. The configured reader uses Google Drive read-only access; the writer uses access to files created or opened with the application. The utility's workflow is restricted to its configured archive locations; a read-only OAuth permission is not itself limited to those folders.

Purpose and transfer. Files and metadata are used to copy, verify and recover the owner's material. File bytes are transferred between the owner's devices and Google Drive. The file-copy utility does not sell data or send file contents to advertising, analytics or AI services. These public pages contain no archive files or credentials.

Credentials and access. Google OAuth credentials are kept in the owner's protected local configuration, separate from these public pages. Existing Drive ownership and sharing settings govern access. Authorisation can be revoked through the Google Account's connected-app controls. Revocation stops future authorised access; it does not itself erase files already stored in Drive.

Retention and deletion. Recovery files remain in the owner's configured storage until managed or removed by the owner or an explicitly approved retention process. The current notice makes no guaranteed backup-frequency, version-history or restore claim. The owner can inspect and remove retained copies in Drive; Google may retain data according to its own policies.
