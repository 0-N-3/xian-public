Xian Personal Archive

A personal utility for maintaining recovery copies of the owner's saved archive files in Google Drive.

This is a private-use tool, not a public storage service. Its information pages do not provide access to the archive.

The utility runs on the owner's devices. Google authorisation is used to inspect configured archive locations and maintain app-managed copies. The current workflow copies selected manuscript collections; whole-archive protection is being developed and is not yet commissioned.

Read the Privacy page for how the utility handles Google Drive data. Support: rainer.wessels@gmail.com.
